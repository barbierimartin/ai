Case Studies

Design Portfolio

Marketing and brand projects powered by AI — from generative photography to landing pages, email systems and full campaigns.

01 — AI Photography

Padel Collections

My Role

AI creative director — turned a single product image into a full photoshoot, ad creative, and landing page.

Software

Generative AI (Midjourney, ComfyUI) for photography and ad variations, Figma for the landing page build.

Process

One product photo, zero physical shoot. Generated a full lifestyle photoshoot with AI, then built ad creative and a landing page around the same imagery. Turnaround dropped from weeks to days, with a full asset library ready for every future drop.

02 — D2C / Email

Kinfolk Outdoors

My Role

D2C designer — PDP photography, full email marketing system, and Shopify template design.

Software

Shopify for the custom theme and templates, Figma for a modular email component library, AI image generation for product photography.

Process

Built a reusable Figma component system so new email sends — park lunch promos, seasonal drops, product launches — could be assembled in hours instead of days. Paired with refreshed PDP imagery and a custom Shopify template, giving the brand a self-sufficient content system it could keep running without outside help.

03 — Fintech / Growth

Chainpay

My Role

Marketing designer — email campaigns, paid ad creative, and landing pages.

Software

Figma for the shared design system and layouts, email platform templates for campaign builds, native specs for Meta and Google ad creative.

Process

Extended the app's existing brand system into every growth channel — email, paid ads, and landing pages built on the same visual logic that powered in-app trust. In fintech, trust is the conversion mechanism, so every surface was built to reduce friction and signal security. Result: a cohesive brand presence from first ad impression to landing page conversion.

04 — CRO

KitchenAid

My Role

UX designer — research, concept development, and A/B testing.

Software

Figma for concepting within KitchenAid's existing design system, live A/B testing tools to validate against real traffic.

Process

Promotions weren't reaching users at the right moment. Proposed a "Promo Drawer" carousel — faced pushback, so instead of compromising, pushed for A/B testing to let the data decide. It did: +60% in sale-page visits, +13% lift in orders.

05 — UX

Whirlpool

My Role

Lead UX designer — research synthesis, concept development, and user testing.

Software

Figma for concept development, live user testing tools to validate against a constrained budget with no new features or content allowed.

Process

Rich internal data, well-defined personas — the work was ruthless prioritization. Same content, restructured around real user needs. Two concepts went to live testing; Option A won, delivering clearer navigation, faster paths to purchase, and stronger brand perception.

06 — Design System

Bobcat

My Role

Solo lead: audit, governance definition, full rebuild, and documentation.

Software

Figma — variables-based component architecture, shared libraries, and Do's-and-Don'ts documentation.

Process

Full component inventory first — mapped what existed, what was live, what could go. Rebuilt on a variable-based foundation with per-component Do's and Don'ts, so teams could use it correctly without asking. The result: a scalable, living system any stakeholder can navigate — fragmented turned into shared.

07 — Fintech

Traders Central

My Role

Design lead and team lead — recruited and mentored 5 designers while owning product, campaigns, and brand system.

Software

Figma as the single source of truth across a distributed team, shared libraries to keep product UI, campaigns, and local markets in sync.

Process

160K+ active users, multiple markets — consistency was non-negotiable. Built a visual language that stretched across product UI, global campaigns, and local markets. Strategy stayed centralized; execution was delegated for real team ownership. Scaled across international markets with a coherent identity, continuously improved through testing.
