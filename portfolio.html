07

Figma Automation

Challenge

Automate repetitive documentation and asset export tasks to reduce manual effort and errors.

Solution

Designed and implemented custom Figma automations using Zapier, including a Slack integration that sends real-time alerts for new file edits and a mass-export workflow that automatically delivers assets from Figma to Google Drive.

These automations significantly reduced friction and time spent on repetitive tasks. The Google Drive export workflow also minimized the risk of accidental file overwrites and asset misplacement, improving overall reliability and team efficiency.
